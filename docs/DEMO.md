# Demo

1. Run `python scripts/run_app.py` and open [MedFire Watch](http://127.0.0.1:8501).
2. Explore Fire danger, search Tunis and Marseille, inspect weather and toggle observations.
3. Open Test, choose license-cleared imagery and inspect annotated boxes.
4. Select a video in Video studio, play it, seek along the detection timeline and download the export.
5. Open Live camera for a visible detection, then review delivery status under Alerts.
