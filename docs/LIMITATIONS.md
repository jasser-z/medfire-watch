# Limitations

- Satellite observations are intermittent and can be delayed or obscured by clouds. A marker does not establish a current confirmed wildfire or its perimeter; no marker does not establish safety.
- Fire-weather danger describes environmental conditions, not guaranteed ignition or a calibrated ignition probability. Provider classes and unavailable issue times are labelled explicitly.
- Forecast spatial detail does not increase with zoom. Local Open-Meteo context is separate from the Copernicus class.
- The EOX 2025 background is a dated mosaic, not live satellite imagery.
- Camera detections may confuse reflections, haze and clouds with smoke or fire. Visibility, camera coverage, networking and Telegram delivery limit alerts.
- The software supports human investigation and does not replace emergency-service verification. No worldwide seconds-from-ignition promise is made.
- HTTP and unit tests do not establish physical camera compatibility or browser timing/fullscreen performance on another machine. Test those flows on the target device.
