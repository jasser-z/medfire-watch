Map guide

Use Controls to turn each layer on or off.

  1. Understand the weather colours

    They show conditions favourable to fire and its spread. A high danger level does not mean a fire is burning. Uncoloured areas may have no data. Right-click a place on the map to see its current weather.

  2. Satellite heat detections · selected time window

    These VIIRS points show observed heat. Choose the last hour, 3, 6, 12 or 24 hours in Controls; the default is 24 hours. Short windows may be empty because satellites do not observe continuously. Zoom in and click a point for its time and location. Numbered circles group observations, not confirmed fires. Heat can also come from industry or other sources. Satellites can miss fires.

Drone camera · planned deployment

The intended setup connects a drone's live camera feed to Live camera. MedFire analyses the images for fire and smoke and sends a snapshot to Telegram when an alert triggers, so an operator can review it.

The PC webcam currently stands in for the drone during testing. Connecting and validating the drone feed is the next integration step. Drone flights must be authorised and coordinated with the response team.
